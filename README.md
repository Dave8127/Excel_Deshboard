# 📊 Super Store Sales & Profit Dashboard

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores Super Store sales data using Microsoft Excel. It
combines data review, sales and profit analysis, PivotTables, advanced
formulas, and a dashboard to present business insights in a clear,
visual format.

The workbook is designed to turn raw order-level data into useful
information for understanding sales performance across product
categories, sub-categories, regions, and time.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

-   Organize and review the raw Super Store dataset.
-   Check data quality, including duplicate and missing values.
-   Analyze sales and profit across different product categories and
    sub-categories.
-   Summarize data with PivotTables.
-   Apply Excel lookup and other advanced formulas.
-   Present key findings through a visual dashboard.

## 📁 Workbook Structure

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Worksheet                           Purpose
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  **Dataset**                         Order-level data, including order
                                      and shipping dates, customer
                                      details, geography, product
                                      information, sales, quantity,
                                      discount, profit, shipping cost,
                                      priority, and year.

  **Dashboard**                       Visual overview of sales and profit
                                      performance, including category and
                                      sub-category comparisons.

  **Analysis**                        Data-quality checks, including
                                      duplicate-value and missing-value
                                      review.

  **Pivot_Table**                     PivotTable summaries for comparing
                                      sales and profit by category and
                                      other dimensions.

  **Advance_Formulas**                Practice and application of
                                      advanced Excel formulas, including
                                      lookup functions.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🧰 Tools & Excel Skills

-   **Microsoft Excel**

-   PivotTables

-   Lookup formulas, including VLOOKUP
-   Formula-based analysis
-   Sales and profit comparison

## 📈 Dataset Details

The `Dataset` worksheet contains fields such as:

-   Order ID, order date, ship date, and ship mode
-   Customer name and customer segment
-   State, country, market, and region
-   Product ID, category, sub-category, and product name
-   Sales, quantity, discount, profit, and shipping cost
-   Order priority and year

## 🔎 Analysis Highlights

The workbook brings together several stages of Excel analysis:

1.  **Data quality review** --- checks for duplicate records and missing
    sales values.
2.  **Sales and profit analysis** --- compares performance across
    product groupings.
3.  **PivotTable summaries** --- provides compact summaries to support
    comparisons.
4.  **Advanced formulas** --- demonstrates lookup-based analysis.
5.  **Dashboard reporting** --- presents important comparisons in a
    visual format.

## 💡 Business Value

This type of analysis can help users:

-   Identify product categories and sub-categories contributing to sales
    and profit.
-   Compare performance across business segments.
-   Review data quality before drawing conclusions.
-   Communicate results through a single dashboard instead of manually
    reviewing every row.

## 🚀 How to Use

1.  Download or clone this repository.
2.  Open `Nemisha_ExcelWorkbook.xlsx` in Microsoft Excel.
3.  Review the **Dataset** worksheet to understand the source data.
4.  Explore **Analysis** and **Pivot_Table** for checks and summarized
    results.
5.  Open **Dashboard** for the visual overview.
6.  Visit **Advance_Formulas** to review the formula examples.

> **Note:** Some PivotTable or dashboard elements may behave differently
> in spreadsheet applications other than Microsoft Excel.
